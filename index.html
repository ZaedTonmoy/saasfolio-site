The Problem

You own dozens of tools. Do you know which ones are actually making you money?

Most software buyers are operating blind. You've invested thousands in lifetime deals, subscriptions, and licenses — but you have no idea whether they're paying off.

Problem 01

No ROI visibility

You've spent $10K+ on tools but can't tell which ones returned a single dollar. Every purchase is a guess, and every renewal is a leap of faith.

Problem 02

Spreadsheet chaos

Your tracking "system" is a Google Sheet with missing columns, outdated numbers, and no way to calculate breakeven, MRR, or profit per tool.

Problem 03

Zero portfolio thinking

You buy tools individually but never step back to see the whole picture — total invested, total revenue, overall portfolio performance.

The Solution

SaaSfolio turns your tool collection into a managed portfolio.

The first purpose-built dashboard for tracking the financial performance of your software investments. Add every tool you own and get real-time visibility into ROI, revenue, profit, and payback timelines.

Solution 01

Portfolio Dashboard

See your total invested amount, total revenue, net profit, and overall ROI at a glance. Know exactly where your portfolio stands — updated automatically.

Solution 02

Per-Tool ROI

Every tool gets its own financial profile: purchase price, revenue generated, profit, ROI percentage, breakeven status, and payback timeline.

Solution 03

Revenue Intelligence

Track one-time sales and recurring subscriptions separately. Monitor MRR, forecast payback timelines, and identify growth engines versus dead weight.

How It Works

From signup to portfolio clarity in three steps.

Add your tools

Import or manually add every software tool you've purchased. Include the purchase price, deal type, and any relevant notes. Takes about five minutes.

Log your revenue

Record revenue as it comes in — one-time sales, recurring subscriptions, or client payments. SaaSfolio calculates ROI, profit, and breakeven automatically.

See the full picture

Your portfolio dashboard lights up with performance metrics, growth charts, and actionable insights. Know what's working, what's not, and where to invest next.
