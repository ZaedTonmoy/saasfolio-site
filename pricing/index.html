Common questions about pricing.

You'll be prompted to upgrade to Pro or Agency. Your data is safe — upgrading simply unlocks the ability to add more tools and access the premium features. You can always stay on Free with your 10 tracked tools if you choose.
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. If you downgrade, you'll have access to your current plan's features immediately. If you're on a paid plan and downgrade to Free, we'll credit any unused subscription time to your account for future upgrades.
Yes. Both Pro and Agency plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. After 14 days, your plan will be paused if you don't add payment information. If you add a payment method during your trial, billing will begin on day 15.
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on annual plans. If you're not satisfied with SaaSfolio, contact our support team within 30 days of your purchase and we'll issue a full refund. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime with no refund, but you won't be charged for the following month.
We're committed to keeping SaaSfolio affordable. If we adjust pricing in the future, we'll give all existing customers at least 60 days' notice. Any price increases won't apply to current subscribers — you'll keep your current rate as long as you maintain your subscription.
