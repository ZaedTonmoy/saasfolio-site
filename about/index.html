ABOUT

Built by software investors, for software investors.

SaaSfolio started as a spreadsheet.

Like most of you, we'd accumulated a portfolio of software tools — lifetime deals from AppSumo, subscriptions for our projects, licenses we resold to clients. The collection grew, but our understanding of it didn't. We knew we'd spent a lot. We had a vague sense that some tools were profitable. But we couldn't quantify any of it.

So we built a spreadsheet. Then the spreadsheet broke under its own weight. Then we built SaaSfolio.

SaaSfolio is the software investment dashboard we wished existed when we were tracking 50 tools across three spreadsheets and a notes app. It brings portfolio-level financial thinking to a space that desperately needs it. Because software purchases aren't just expenses — they're investments. And investments deserve measurement.

Today, SaaSfolio serves LTD buyers, agency owners, indie hackers, SaaS founders, and resellers who take their software portfolios seriously. Our goal is to evolve into the financial operating system for anyone who buys, sells, or manages software as a business asset.

We're just getting started. And we're building in public.

Our values.

Clarity over complexity.

Every feature exists to make a number clearer, a decision easier, or a workflow simpler. We don't add features for the sake of feature lists.

Built for investors, not users.

We think of software buyers as investors. Our language, our metrics, and our design reflect that. ROI, not 'usage.' Profit, not 'engagement.' Portfolio, not 'list.'

Transparent by default.

We publish our roadmap. We share our metrics. We build in public. If you invest in SaaSfolio, you should be able to track that investment too.
